How this comparison is calculated

Only the general rate moves from capital value (CV) to land value (LV). The base sector targeted rate stays on CV. The council scenario keeps the residential/commercial funding split at 51:49. Water is excluded from both sides; the old water-inclusive model is not used.

For standard residential estimates, the following coefficients (GST included) were fitted to official council lookup results, rather than taken from a published full-precision rate schedule:

  • CV scenario: CV × .
  • LV scenario: LV × + CV × .
  • Annual saving = CV scenario − LV scenario. A negative saving is shown as an increase. Weekly change = annual change ÷ 52.

All annual and weekly figures are calculated when this file is built; search only retrieves saved results. Estimates round each annual scenario to cents before comparing. The council may round components differently. Results marked “Council amounts checked” use the two displayed council amounts captured on 29 October 2026. Other results use the fitted formula and source-file valuations. These are estimates, not a council quote.

Rural, farmland and vacant/derelict categories are referred to the council tool. Commercial and mixed-use accounts are outside this residential dataset. A positive or negative result for an account is not necessarily the change for one dwelling or tenant.
